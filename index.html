About me

I'm a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the University of Peradeniya with a strong pull toward hardware design, digital systems, and the layer where software meets silicon. Whether it's an ARM32 shell running raw syscalls or a production AI SaaS behind Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API, I want to understand the system all the way down.

Alongside coursework, I mentor students in physics at Peradeniya Science Hall and the Thalgaspitiya Education Development Association, and I'm active within the Undergraduates and Young Graduates Association — I enjoy environments where knowledge moves both ways.

I'm motivated by real engineering constraints: production deployment, security, scalability — and by the process of debugging a system until it actually survives contact with real users.
